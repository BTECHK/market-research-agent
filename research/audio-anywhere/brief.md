# Research Brief

**Working name:** Audio Anywhere
**Question:** Is there a viable market for an iOS-first (later desktop) app that reads long documents aloud with audiobook-grade quality and audiobook-style progress saving — starting with Google Docs/PDF/documents (MVP1), later "read anything from anywhere on your phone" (texts, web, email, etc.)?
**Founder's observed pain:** Built-in TTS (Google Docs read-aloud, iOS Speak Screen/Spoken Content) is poor for long documents — e.g. a 20-page briefing: robotic voice, loses place, iOS reader chokes on long content. Speech-to-text has great AI tools (Wispr Flow, Superwhisper); text-to-speech for long-form reading seems underserved.
**Target market:** English-speaking, US-first; iOS primary; professionals/students/knowledge workers, plus accessibility users (dyslexia, ADHD, low vision).
**Decision to inform:** (1) Build / don't build; (2) where to position vs. incumbents (niche to own); (3) pricing & monetization; (4) go-to-market.
**Hypotheses to test (challenge, don't confirm):**
- H1: Built-in and existing TTS readers handle long documents badly and people complain about it (not just the founder).
- H2: No strong AI-powered, audiobook-style document reader already dominates the App Store.
- H3: A focused niche (long docs + progress saving + Google Docs/PDF import) can be owned rather than competing broadly.
- H4: People will pay a subscription for this.
**Depth:** Deep dive — McKinsey/Bain/BCG-style, adversarial ("not looking for agreement").
**Date:** 2026-09-25
